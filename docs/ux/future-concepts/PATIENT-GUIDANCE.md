# Future concept: Patient Guidance

- Status: Future only
- Current decision: Do not implement
- Current routes/navigation: None

## Preserved concept

The Lovable prototype explored a patient-guidance experience covering Utah program steps, caregivers, transport, dosage forms, restrictions, and common questions. It may be reconsidered as a separate content product later.

## Requirements before reconsideration

- A named product and content owner.
- Legal and medical-content review.
- Authoritative Utah source links for every time-sensitive claim.
- A visible last-reviewed date and maintenance cadence.
- Clear separation between general information and medical advice.
- Analytics or research supporting the user need.
- A route, search, and navigation strategy approved independently of the current redesign.

Until these requirements are met, agents must not add a Guidance link, public route, teaser, or placeholder page.
